# Courses at Sunway College Kathmandu

Sunway College Kathmandu offers degree programs in academic partnership with Birmingham City University, UK. All programs are approved by Nepal's Ministry of Education and carry Tribhuvan University equivalency recognition. Two programs are currently active: BSc (Hons) Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence and BSc (Hons) Business Information Technology. Two additional programs — BSc (Hons) Cybersecurity and MSc Artificial Intelligence — are in the approval process and expected to launch by Autumn 2026.

---

## BSc (Hons) Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence

**Duration:** 4 Years
**Total Credits:** 480
**Timing:** Full Time (7 AM to 1 PM)
**Program Coordinator:** Mr. Rupak Koirala

This program is designed for students who want to build a strong foundation in computer science while specializing in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science. Students from any academic background — Science, Management, Humanities, or Education — can apply, and no prior programming experience is required. The course begins from the basics and progressively deepens into advanced AI topics by the final year.

The program is entirely practical in its emphasis. Students work with industry-standard tools throughout, complete real-world projects in every year, and spend an entire dedicated year on professional placement with a company before returning to complete their final year.

### Year One

The first year builds the core foundations. Students study Computer Systems, Website Design and Development, Data Structures and Algorithms, Computer Programming, Introduction to Artificial Intelligence, and an Innovation Project. The Innovation Project introduces students to the idea of building something meaningful from the very beginning.

Tools used in this year include TinkerCad, Jupyter Notebook, VS Code, Google Colab, Python, and Figma. Non-credit sessions offered alongside include Git and GitHub, React, Data Analysis with Python, and Probability and Statistics.

### Year Two

The second year moves into more advanced territory. Students study Object Oriented Programming, Database and Web Application Development, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Cyber Security, Software Design, and Data Management and Machine Learning Operations.

Tools used include MySQL, NumPy, Seaborn, Sandbox, Lucidchart, Azure, Java, and OpenVAS. Non-credit sessions offered include Node.js, Linear Algebra, and Mobile Programming using Flutter.

### Year Three — Professional Placement Year

The entire third year is a professional placement year worth 120 credits. After completing two years of academic study, students enter the industry and work with a company for a minimum of nine months. The college's dedicated placement cell assists students in securing placements, and students may also find their own placement company with support from the cell.

This year is what makes the degree stand out. Students gain real industry experience, build professional networks, and return to their final year with practical knowledge that enriches their academic work.

### Year Four

The final year brings together everything students have learned and pushes them into advanced, research-level work. Students complete an Individual Honours Project worth 40 credits, which serves as an in-depth capstone of their entire academic journey. They also study Deep Neural Networks and Ethics, Modern Data Stores and Data Protection, Natural Language Processing, and Cloud Computing.

Tools used in this year include Keras, Neo4j, TensorFlow, MongoDB, AWS, and PyTorch.

## BSc (Hons) Business Information Technology

**Duration:** 3 Years
**Total Credits:** 360
**Timing:** Full Time

This program is built for students who want to combine business knowledge with modern information technology. It covers enterprise systems, business intelligence, IT management, digital transformation, and application development. Students without an IT background are fully welcome — the program builds technical and business skills from the ground up.

### Year One

Students study Business Information Systems, Computational Thinking and Professional Development, Computer Systems, Business Information Modelling, Introduction to Programming, and an Innovation Project.

Tools used in this year include TinkerCad, MySQL, Miro, Lucidchart, Jira, and Python.

### Year Two

The second year deepens both technical and business competencies. Students study Database System Development, Business Process Reengineering, Enterprise Systems, Human Computer Interaction, Applied Programming, and an Enterprise Practice Project.

Tools used include Canva, Ari Suite, SAP ERP, Postman, Kubernetes, GitHub, Docker, and Sketch.

### Year Three

The final year brings together strategic, analytical, and technical disciplines. Students complete an Individual Project worth 40 credits, and also study Strategic Information System Alignment, Business Intelligence, Application Solution Development, and Consultancy and IT Management.

Tools used in this year include Webflow, Tableau, TensorFlow, Dataverse, Power Platform, and Plotly.

## Non-Credit Courses

Alongside their degree modules, students at Sunway can attend a rich menu of non-credit courses designed to expand practical skills beyond the formal curriculum. These are offered free of charge as part of the college's commitment to industry-ready education.

Available non-credit courses include Data Analysis with Python, Linear Algebra, Robotic Process Automation, Mobile Programming using Flutter, Probability and Statistics, Large Language Models, Node.js, Graphic Designing, Power BI, Machine Learning, React, Git and GitHub, UI/UX Design, Prompt Engineering, Generative AI, SQL for Data Science, Pandas for Data Wrangling, IoT with TinkerCad, Natural Language Processing Basics, and computer Vision.

## Key Differences Between BSc AI and BSc BIT

The BSc AI program is the right choice for students drawn to artificial intelligence, machine learning, software engineering, data science, and deep technical research. It is a four-year degree that includes a dedicated placement year and culminates in an honours research project.

The BSc BIT program suits students who want to work at the intersection of business and technology — roles like business analyst, IT manager, enterprise systems consultant, or digital transformation specialist. It is a three-year degree with a strong applied project component in the final year.

Both programs are internationally affiliated through Birmingham City University, both lead to globally recognized degrees, and both are recognized in Nepal through Ministry of Education approval and TU equivalency.
