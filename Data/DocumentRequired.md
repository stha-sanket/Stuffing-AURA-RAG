# Documents Required for Admission

When applying to Sunway College Kathmandu, applicants must submit a complete set of documents to be considered for admission. All documents should be prepared in advance to avoid delays in the process.

---

## Academic Documents

Applicants are required to provide their Grade 10 marksheet along with the completion certificate and character certificate issued by their school. For secondary education, the Grade 11 marksheet must also be submitted. For Grade 12, applicants must provide the marksheet, the provisional certificate, the migration certificate, and the character certificate from their institution.

---

## Identification

A copy of the applicant's citizenship certificate or passport is required. Either document is acceptable for identity verification purposes.

---

## English Proficiency

Students who received a grade of B+ or below in their Grade 12 English subject are required to submit an English Proficiency Test marksheet as part of their application. This applies to students sitting the BCU English Proficiency Test or providing scores from internationally recognized tests such as IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, or ELLTS. Students who received Grade A or above in Grade 12 English are fully exempted from this requirement.

---

## Recommendation Letter

At least one recommendation letter from the applicant's previous school is required. This letter should speak to the student's academic character and readiness for higher education.

---

## Photographs

Two passport-size photographs must be submitted along with the application documents.

---

## Notes

All documents should be recent and accurate. If any documents are unavailable at the time of application — such as a final Grade 12 marksheet for students still awaiting results — applicants should contact the Admissions Department directly at admission@sunway.edu.np or by calling 9823047066 to discuss provisional admission options. Applications may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis by the course committee at Birmingham City University.
