# Sunway College Kathmandu

## About Sunway College Kathmandu

Sunway College Kathmandu is a leading IT and AI-focused college in Nepal, offering internationally recognized undergraduate programs in partnership with Birmingham City University (BCU), United Kingdom. The college is known for its industry-oriented curriculum, practical learning approach, strong placement support, and global academic standards.

Sunway College offers the **BSc (Hons) Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence** program, designed to equip students with the skills required in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Machine Learning, Software Development, and emerging technologies.

The college has over **16 years of educational excellence** and has built a reputation for producing industry-ready graduates through international collaborations, research opportunities, and professional placements.

## Academic Partner

### Birmingham City University (BCU), United Kingdom

Birmingham City University is the academic partner of Sunway College Kathmandu, it is a renowned UK university recognized for its strong industry connections and practice-based learning approach.

**Key Highlights:**
- Ranked **601–800** in the **Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2024**
- Over **31,000 students**
- Students from **100+ countries**
- Located in Birmingham, United Kingdom
- Strong focus on employability and practical education
- Extensive industry partnerships and research opportunities

Degrees offered at Sunway College Kathmandu are awarded through Birmingham City University (BCU), UK.
