# Eligibility Criteria for Admission

Sunway College Kathmandu welcomes applications from students across all academic streams including Science, Management, Humanities, Education, and others. Meeting the eligibility criteria below is the primary requirement for admission consideration.

---

## BSc (Hons) Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence

### Academic Requirements

Students who completed Grade 12 through the National Examination Board (NEB) or an equivalent board must have achieved an aggregate CGPA of 2.6 or higher.

Students applying with A-Level qualifications must have a minimum of 3.0 credits with at least a grade of D or above, which corresponds to 112 UCAS points or above.

### English Proficiency Requirements

English proficiency requirements vary depending on the grade the applicant received in their Grade 12 English subject.

Students who received Grade A or above in Grade 12 English are fully exempted from any additional English proficiency testing.

Students who received Grade B or B+ are required to sit the BCU English Proficiency Test and must achieve a minimum score of 5.5 in both the Listening and Speaking components.

Students who received below Grade B in Grade 12 English must demonstrate proficiency through a BCU-recognized international English test. Accepted tests include IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, and ELLTS. The required score is an overall 6.0 with a minimum of 5.5 in all individual bands.

---

## BSc (Hons) Business Information Technology

### Academic Requirements

The academic requirements for the BIT program are the same as for the AI program. Students applying through NEB or an equivalent board need a minimum aggregate CGPA of 2.6. Students applying with A-Level qualifications need a minimum of 3.0 credits with at least grade D or above, equivalent to 112 UCAS points or above.

### English Proficiency Requirements

Applicants must meet one of the following two options.

Option A requires a minimum of Grade B or above in Grade 12 English combined with a passing score of at least 5.5 in each band of the BCU EPT Listening and Speaking Test.

Option B requires a BCU-recognized English test result — IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, or ELLTS — showing an overall score of 6.0 with no individual band falling below 5.5.

---

## Applicants Who Do Not Meet the Minimum GPA

Students who fall below the minimum CGPA requirement of 2.6 are still encouraged to apply. All such applications are reviewed individually by the Course Committee at Birmingham City University and may be approved based on the broader profile of the applicant. Meeting the minimum is the standard pathway, but exceptions are possible on a case-by-case basis.

---

## A Note on Stream

No specific academic stream from Grade 12 is required for either program. Whether a student studied Science, Management, Humanities, or Education at the plus-two level, they are equally eligible to apply as long as they meet the GPA and English requirements. No prior programming or computing knowledge is required to join either program.
